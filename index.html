signature features
up in a minute

One command — and it's running

One command to install and start, then scan the QR. If Node and tmux are ready, your phone is in the terminal in about a minute.

on by default

Zero install — with a native-app feel

No App Store, no client. Add the PWA to your Home Screen for a full-screen, native-like experience on iPhone and Android, including push notifications.

on by default

Your workspace survives a restart

Recover your tmux workspace after a restart — sessions, windows, panes and safe agent context. Existing sessions stay untouched; restore from phone or CLI.

experimental · opt-in

Chat view — the session as a conversation

Turn a Claude terminal into chat: Markdown bubbles, colored tool diffs and tap-to-answer cards. Keep the terminal and switch views whenever you need.

on by default

Agent usage, always in view

Claude and Codex usage at a glance: limits, reset countdowns and burn pace. The latest host-wide state stays shared across every connected device.

on by default

Real-time terminal, the right keyboard everywhere

Live terminal everywhere: resilient streaming on mobile, full physical-keyboard input and native copy/paste on desktop.

on by default

A git client in your hand

VS Code-style Git on your phone: working changes, history, branches and full-screen colored diffs. Multi-repo and read-only, so your tree stays untouched.

on by default

Split, close, and map panes from your phone

Control real tmux layouts from your phone: see every pane, split, close and jump between them. Every change appears instantly on desktop.

on by default

One previewer for sites and local folders

Preview URLs by phone or computer proxy, or open an isolated static folder from the same address row—with phone/desktop views and layout-preserving zoom.

on by default

Docs you can read — or hear — and files both ways

Open terminal paths as readable documents, with Markdown, zoom and read-aloud highlighting. Upload, download and share files in either direction.

needs setup

Reach it anywhere — direct, zero-config, or self-hosted

Connect your way: direct on LAN or public IP, Cloudflare, natapp/cpolar, or your own SSH server. handmux configures the route; the account remains yours.

needs setup

Script push — notify your phone from a script

Push from any script or CI job straight to your phone. Target every device, one tmux session or one device — without a remote handmux service.

free · open-source

Free, open-source, no relay

Free, open-source and self-hosted. No handmux cloud, account or relay sits between your computer and phone — even when you use a supported tunnel.

good to know

  • Voice input: dictate prompts in chat view with optional iFlytek setup.
  • Update alerts: know when a new release is ready, then reload the client from Settings after upgrading.
  • Built for flaky networks: reconnects, falls back and resumes without losing your place.
  • Image & GIF viewer: pinch-zoom, save and share terminal images.
  • Starts at login: bring handmux back automatically after a reboot.
  • Idea capture: keep notes per window and send them into the prompt.

get going · about a minute

Install Node and tmux on your computer; your phone only needs a browser.

npm i -g handmux   # install once
handmux start       # run it, then scan the QR it prints — you're on your phone
